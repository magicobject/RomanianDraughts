Romanian Draughts

Eight kings a side. Jump your own pieces to travel, jump your opponent's to take them.

    Rules in brief
    • Every piece is a king and moves diagonally in any direction.
    • Step one square, or jump over any adjacent piece onto the empty square beyond.
    • Keep jumping with the same piece as long as you like, and stop whenever you choose.
    • Jumped opponent's pieces are taken. Jumping your own takes nothing.
    • Capturing is never compulsory.
    • Win by taking your opponent's last piece or leaving them with no legal move.
