# Romanian Draughts — Rules

## Overview

Romanian Draughts is a two-player draughts variant in which every piece is a king from the start. Each side has 8 kings. Pieces may jump over their own men as well as the opponent's, but only the opponent's pieces are captured. Greg first came across the game while travelling in Romania, hence the name.

## Board and starting position

The game is played on a standard 8×8 draughts board, using only the 32 dark squares.

- Place the board so each player has a light square in their near right-hand corner, as in English draughts and chess.
- Each player sets out 8 kings on the dark squares of their two nearest rows, 4 per row.
- The middle four rows start empty.
- Dark moves first, then players alternate.

## Moves: steps and jumps

On each turn a player moves one of their pieces, either by a single step or by a chain of jumps. Every piece is a king, so all moves can go forwards or backwards.

1. **Step:** move one square diagonally onto an empty adjacent square.
2. **Jump:** hop diagonally over an adjacent piece, of either colour, onto the empty square directly beyond it.
3. **Chain of jumps:** after a jump, the same piece may jump again, in any diagonal direction, as many times as the player likes. The player may stop after any jump.

A turn is either one step or one chain of jumps, never a mix of the two. A piece may not jump the same opponent's piece twice in one turn.

## Capturing

Any opponent's piece that is jumped is captured. Jumping your own piece captures nothing.

- Capturing is never compulsory. A player may decline a capture, or stop a chain before taking everything available.
- Captured pieces are removed when the turn ends, so they cannot be jumped a second time in the same chain.
- A chain may not land on the same square twice in one turn.

## Winning

The winner is the player who makes the last legal move. That happens in one of two ways:

- You capture your opponent's last piece.
- Your opponent has pieces left but no legal step or jump on their turn.

## Differences from English draughts

| Rule | English draughts | Romanian Draughts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pieces per side | 12 men | 8 kings |
| Starting rows | 3 | 2 |
| Direction | Men forwards only until crowned | All pieces any direction |
| Jumping own pieces | Not allowed | Allowed, no capture |
| Compulsory capture | Yes | No |
| Stopping a chain early | Not allowed | Allowed |
| Win | Capture all, or opponent blocked | Same |
